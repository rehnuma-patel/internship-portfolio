    Quick Phishing Checklist

    • Check the sender's real email address, not just the display name.
    • Hover over links to see the actual URL before clicking.
    • Be suspicious of urgency, threats or prizes.
    • Legitimate companies never ask for passwords or card PINs by email.
    • Watch for unexpected attachments, especially .zip, .exe or macro files.
    • If unsure, contact the company using their official website or phone number.
